# Zuzia — product context

**What it is.** The personal site of Zuziâ RodzeN (GitHub: ZuziaDev, brand: Zuzia Inc.). Tagline: "Engineering AI,
Emotion & Imagination".

**Who made it.** A cook in Turkey with a tourism high school background who builds software and AI in their free
time. Self-taught since 2020. Projects: Neuroa (AI assistant and Discord bot), NoteAI (Electron note app), Mirai
(Python generative-AI project).

**Who visits.** Developers who found a repository, members of the Discord community, people considering a
collaboration.

**What they should be able to do.** Understand who this is in seconds; see what they work with and how confident
they are in each tool; browse, filter and open projects and repositories; send a message; switch language (en, ja,
ru, az, tr, kk, ky), light/dark mode and colour pair.

**Voice.** First person, plain, warm, curious. "Can I build this too?" No sales language, no invented numbers.

**Constraints.** Next.js 16 app router, React 19, Tailwind v4, framer-motion, three.js. Contact form posts to
`/api/contact` (Resend). GitHub repositories are fetched client-side from the public API.

Visual decisions live in DESIGN.md.
